This exercise is designed to assess your strategic thinking, architectural knowledge, and
problem-solving skills in a real-world scenario.
While this challenge does not require you to write any code, we encourage you to
leverage your experience with modern backend technologies, cloud platforms
(especially AWS), and AI integration frameworks like LangChain, as well as any
development tools or methodologies you typically employ.
Feel free to explore and propose innovative solutions within the given constraints. Your
ability to articulate complex technical designs, justify your decisions, and anticipate
operational challenges will be key to your success.

Business Context
A B2B platform wants to enable its clients to upload invoices in PDF format (both
supplier and customer). The system should extract and validate key fields, register the
information into an operational database, and enable a conversational interface to query
any invoice (by supplier, date, project, taxes, discrepancies, etc.). The platform should
integrate with an external ERP (e.g., NetSuite / SAP) for reconciliation of documents and
payment statuses.

Scope
● Ingestion: Web + API for PDF upload with minimal metadata.
● Extraction: OCR + LLM/IE for key fields (supplier, ID, date, amounts, taxes).
● Validation: hard business rules, confidence scores, and human-in-the-loop if
confidence is low.
● Persistence: multi-tenant relational DB + PDF storage + provenance.
● Query: structured APIs + conversational interface (RAG + SQL generation).
● ERP Integration: webhooks and batch jobs.
● Audit: tenant logs, change trails, exportable reports.

Assumptions and Constraints
● Load: 150k PDFs/month; peaks of 20 PDFs/s ingestion, 30 QPS for queries.
● SLOs: ingestion+extraction P95 < 8s; structured query P95 < 400ms;
conversational query P95 < 2.5s.
● AI Budget: ≤ USD 18k/month.
● Accuracy targets: ≥ 98% (totals/dates), ≥ 96% (taxes/currency), ≥ 95% (supplier
ID).
● Multi-country: diverse tax schemes and currencies.
● Security/compliance: multi-tenant, encryption, PII redaction, regional data
residency.
● AI provider instability: assume 20% rate limits/timeouts during peak.

Deliverables (NO CODING REQUIRED)
1) System Design Document (max. 10 pages)
● Architecture with diagrams (C4 + sequence flows).
● Key decisions and trade-offs
● Data model and provenance.
● Reliability and cost estimates.
● Security and compliance approach.
● Observability strategy.
2) API Specifications (max. 3 pages, JSON examples)
● /invoices/upload, /invoices/{id}, /invoices/search, /assist/query, ERP webhooks.
3) AI/IE & Evaluation Plan (max. 3 pages)
● Extraction, validation, conversational layer, quality metrics.
4) MLOps & Operations (max. 2 pages)
● Versioning, drift detection, backpressure handling, cost controls.
